TERMS OF SERVICE

Stuff & Puff | Chengalpattu (Near GH Bus Stop, Chengalpattu)

1. Pickup & Preparation Timings

Stuff & Puff operates as a roadside pushcart takeaway food outlet operating between 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM. Orders require a minimum preparation buffer of 20 minutes from the time of booking.

2. Pickup Token & Verification

Each confirmed paid order receives a unique Order Number and Pickup Token (e.g. SP1001). Customers must present their pickup token upon arrival at the cart to collect their fresh order.

3. 15-Minute Grace Period

Please collect your order around your selected pickup time. A 15-minute grace period is provided; after that, food freshness/hotness cannot be guaranteed as items are made fresh to order.

4. Parcel & Packaging

A parcel charge of ₹5 per item quantity marked for parcel applies to cover food-grade takeaway packaging.
