PRIVACY POLICY

Stuff & Puff | Chengalpattu (Near GH Bus Stop, Chengalpattu)

1. Information We Collect

To process pre-booking takeaway orders, we only collect your Name and Mobile Number, along with selected food items and pickup time. We do not require account passwords or store unnecessary personal data.

2. Purpose of Collection

Your mobile number is strictly used to identify your order, generate your unique pickup token (e.g. SP1001), and verify order collection at our pickup counter.

3. Payment Information

All online payments are securely processed through authorized Indian payment gateways supporting UPI and Net Banking. We never store bank accounts, PINs, or sensitive payment credentials on our servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
